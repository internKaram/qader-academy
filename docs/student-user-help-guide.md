# Qader Academy — Student User Help Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Qader Academy**! 🎓

We're glad you're here. This platform is designed to help you build real skills at your own pace — from your very first course, through every lesson and quiz, all the way to earning a certificate you can proudly share.

This guide walks you through everything you need to know to use Qader Academy with confidence, step by step. Whether this is your first time on the platform or you just want a quick refresher, you'll find clear instructions here for:

- Creating your account and signing in
- Finding and enrolling in the right courses for you
- Tracking your progress as you learn
- Completing lessons and quizzes
- Downloading your certificate
- Getting help whenever you need it

Let's get started!

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating Your Account

1. Go to the Qader Academy homepage.
2. Click the **Sign Up** button, usually located in the top-right corner of the page.
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email Address**
   - **Password** (choose a strong password you'll remember)
4. Click **Create Account** to submit the form.
5. Check your email inbox for a welcome message. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your **Spam** or **Promotions** folder.

> 💡 **Tip:** Use an email address you check regularly — you'll receive important updates about your courses and certificates there.

### 2.2 Logging In

1. Click the **Log In** button on the homepage.
2. Enter the **email address** and **password** you used to register.
3. Click **Log In** to access your account.
4. You'll be taken directly to your **Dashboard** — your personal learning home base.

### 2.3 Forgot Your Password?

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Open the email you receive and follow the link to set a new password.

## 3. Your Learning Journey

### 3.1 Browsing the Course Catalog

1. From the top navigation menu, click **Courses** to open the full course catalog.
2. Use the **Category Filter** on the left (or top, on mobile) to narrow down courses by subject — for example, *Programming*, *Business*, or *Design*.
3. Use the **Search Bar** if you already know the course title you're looking for.
4. Click on any course card to view its full details, including:
   - Course description
   - Instructor information
   - Number of lessons
   - Estimated duration

> 💡 **Tip:** Take a moment to read the course description and lesson list before enrolling — this helps you choose the course that best matches your goals.

### 3.2 Enrolling in a Course

1. Open the course page for the course you'd like to take.
2. Review the course details to make sure it's the right fit.
3. Click the **Enroll Button**.
4. Once enrolled, the course will automatically appear in the **My Courses** section of your **Dashboard**.

### 3.3 Tracking Your Progress

Your **Dashboard** is where you can see everything about your learning journey at a glance.

- Each enrolled course displays a **Progress Bar**, showing the percentage of lessons you've completed.
- Click on any course card in your **Dashboard** to jump straight back into your next lesson.
- Look out for the **Study Reminder Card** at the top of your **Dashboard** — it gently nudges you with your exact progress and the next lesson to tackle, so you never lose your place if you've been away for a while. You can dismiss it at any time by clicking the **✕** icon.

> 💡 **Tip:** Check your **Dashboard** regularly to stay motivated and see how far you've come!

## 4. Lesson & Quiz Experience

### 4.1 Navigating Lessons

1. From your **Dashboard**, click on a course to open its lesson list.
2. Lessons are arranged in order — we recommend completing them in sequence for the best learning experience.
3. Click on a lesson title to open it.
4. Use the **Video Player** controls to:
   - **Play / Pause** the lesson video
   - Adjust the **Volume**
   - Use the **Playback Speed** option if you'd like to watch faster or slower
   - Drag the **Progress Slider** to rewind or skip ahead
5. Once you finish watching or reading a lesson, click **Mark as Complete** to move on to the next one.

> 💡 **Tip:** If a lesson includes downloadable resources, look for the **Attachments** section below the video player.

### 4.2 Taking Quizzes

1. At the end of a lesson or module, you'll find a **Quiz** section.
2. Click **Start Quiz** when you're ready to begin.
3. Read each question carefully and select your answer before moving to the next question.
4. Once you've answered all the questions, click **Submit Quiz**.

**Understanding Your Results:**

- Your score is shown immediately after submission as a percentage.
- Each quiz has a **Passing Threshold** (commonly 70%, but check the specific quiz instructions, as this may vary by course).
- If you meet or exceed the passing threshold, the quiz — and the related lesson — will be marked **Complete** ✅.
- If you score below the passing threshold, don't worry! You can review the lesson material and click **Retake Quiz** to try again.

> 💡 **Tip:** Take your time. There's no penalty for reviewing a lesson more than once before attempting the quiz.

## 5. Certification

Once you've completed **all lessons and passed all quizzes** in a course, you've earned your certificate!

1. Go to your **Dashboard**.
2. Find the completed course — it will be marked **100% Complete**.
3. Click **View Certificate**.
4. On the certificate page, click the **Download Certificate** button to save a copy (usually as a PDF) to your device.
5. You can also click **Share** to post your achievement directly to your social media or professional profiles.

> 🎉 **Congratulations in advance!** Completing a course and earning your certificate is a real accomplishment — be proud of the effort you put in.

---

## 6. Support

If you run into any trouble — a technical issue, a question about a course, or anything else — we're here to help.

1. Click **Contact Us**, found in the footer at the bottom of any page.
2. Fill in the short form with:
   - Your **name and email**
   - A brief **description of your issue**
3. Click **Send Message**.
4. Our support team will get back to you as soon as possible by email.

> 💡 **Tip:** Include as much detail as possible (such as the course name or a screenshot of the issue) — this helps our team assist you faster.
